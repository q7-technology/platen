A Zebra printer has never heard of a PNG. It knows dots. So: decode the base64, hand it to Pillow, shrink it to the exact dot size of the box on the label, dither to pure black and white, then pack those dots eight to a byte.

The browser gets its answer straight away — nobody watches a spinner while twelve labels crawl out. And the worker now does exactly one thing: open a socket and write. Nothing left in the job can fail for an interesting reason.

It also makes Cancel honest: the flag is checked between labels, so stopping means the next label, not the next batch.
